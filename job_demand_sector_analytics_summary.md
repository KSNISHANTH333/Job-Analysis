## 6. Regional Disparities Analysis
Regional comparison between **Bengaluru Urban** and remaining Karnataka districts illustrates heavy spatial concentration of formal industrial and technology employment. However, analysts must observe the following methodological guardrails:
- **Aggregate Volume vs. Individual Opportunity:** Aggregate counts reflect enterprise cluster sizes. A higher district count does not automatically guarantee higher individual probability of obtaining employment.
- **Urban Core Boundary Effects:** Bengaluru Urban represents the administrative city center. Many manufacturing facilities and industrial estates have spilled over into Bengaluru Rural and Ramanagara.

## 7. Methodological Limitations and Quality Caveats
1. **Informal Sector Underrepresentation:** Official employment exchange registrations and factory returns cover formal registered establishments. The vast informal and gig economy is not captured in administrative vacancy returns.
2. **Job Postings as Recruitment Proxies:** Online job advertisement counts reflect digital hiring intent and employer search effort, not net job creation. They suffer from duplication, scraping lags, and tech-sector bias.
3. **Survey Sampling Variability:** Household survey indicators (such as PLFS employment rates) represent probabilistic statistical estimates subject to standard errors and reference-period smoothing.
4. **No Cross-Indicator Aggregation:** Job postings, reported vacancies, factory counts, and employment rates have distinct mathematical foundations and cannot be legitimately summed together.

## 8. Data Sources and Provenance Register
### Vacancies Notified to Employment Exchanges by Broad Economic Activity
- **Publishing Organisation:** Directorate General of Employment (DGE), Ministry of Labour and Employment, Government of India
- **Official Link:** [https://dge.gov.in/dge/reports_publications](https://dge.gov.in/dge/reports_publications)
- **Indicator Classified:** `Actual vacancy count` (Number of vacancies)
- **Geographic Scope:** Karnataka and All-India
- **Methodology Note:** Covers only vacancies voluntarily or statutorily notified under the Employment Exchanges (Compulsory Notification of Vacancies) Act, 1959. Primarily reflects public sector and formal organized private sector vacancies. Significant under-reporting exists for the informal economy and certain modern tech-enabled private services.

### Vacancies Notified to Employment Exchanges by Occupational Division
- **Publishing Organisation:** Directorate General of Employment (DGE), Ministry of Labour and Employment, Government of India
- **Official Link:** [https://dge.gov.in/dge/reports_publications](https://dge.gov.in/dge/reports_publications)
- **Indicator Classified:** `Actual vacancy count` (Number of vacancies)
- **Geographic Scope:** Karnataka
- **Methodology Note:** Occupational classifications are recorded based on employer descriptions at the time of vacancy registration. Skilled agricultural and informal service occupations are underrepresented due to reliance on formal notifications.

### District-wise Registered Working Factories and Workers Employed in Karnataka
- **Publishing Organisation:** Directorate of Economics and Statistics (DES), Government of Karnataka
- **Official Link:** [https://des.karnataka.gov.in/english](https://des.karnataka.gov.in/english)
- **Indicator Classified:** `Employment count` (Number of workers)
- **Geographic Scope:** Karnataka (All 31 administrative districts, with explicit reporting for Bengaluru Urban)
- **Methodology Note:** Pertains strictly to registered manufacturing factories complying with the Factories Act (employing 10+ workers with power or 20+ workers without power). Excludes IT/ITES establishments registered under the Shops and Commercial Establishments Act, construction sites, and unorganized micro-enterprises.

### Quarterly Employment Survey (QES) - Nine Selected Non-Farm Sectors
- **Publishing Organisation:** Labour Bureau, Ministry of Labour and Employment, Government of India
- **Official Link:** [https://labourbureau.gov.in/qes](https://labourbureau.gov.in/qes)
- **Indicator Classified:** `Employment estimate` (Thousands of workers)
- **Geographic Scope:** India / Major States (including Karnataka)
- **Methodology Note:** Restricted to establishments employing 10 or more workers (the organized segment of the 9 non-farm sectors). Sampling frame based on Sixth Economic Census (2013-14). Informal unorganized units (<10 workers) are covered separately under the Area Frame survey and are not included in these figures.

### Periodic Labour Force Survey (PLFS) - Distribution of Usually Working Persons by Industry
- **Publishing Organisation:** National Sample Survey Office (NSSO), MoSPI, Government of India
- **Official Link:** [https://mospi.gov.in/periodic-labour-force-survey-plfs](https://mospi.gov.in/periodic-labour-force-survey-plfs)
- **Indicator Classified:** `Employment rate` (Percentage (%))
- **Geographic Scope:** Karnataka and All-India
- **Methodology Note:** Derived from household sample surveys. Subject to sampling errors and survey non-response. Reflects the usual status of employment over a 365-day reference period; temporary and seasonal fluctuations may be smoothed out.

### Aggregated Online Job Postings by Technology and Digital Sector
- **Publishing Organisation:** Public Job Aggregation Research Digest (Documented Labour Market Observatory)
- **Official Link:** [https://labour.gov.in/data-portal/online-job-postings](https://labour.gov.in/data-portal/online-job-postings)
- **Indicator Classified:** `Job-posting count` (Number of job postings)
- **Geographic Scope:** Karnataka (Bengaluru Urban and Tier-2 hubs)
- **Methodology Note:** A proxy measure of recruitment demand. Does not represent all new job openings or net employment generation. Heavily skewed toward urban, white-collar, and technology roles. Subject to portal scraping limitations, re-posting delays, and phantom job advertisements.
